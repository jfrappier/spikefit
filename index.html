F.R.E.S.H. Auto-Regulator

The Fatigue & Readiness Evaluation System for Health tracks your daily jump volume and joint soreness using the Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio (ACWR). If the system detects an unsafe spike in workload, it dynamically intervenes—swapping high-impact plyometrics for explosive, low-impact alternatives to protect your knees from Jumper's Knee.
Note: This advanced sports-science logic is currently in beta and may have occasional bugs as we refine the math.
